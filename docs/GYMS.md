# Worcestershire gym directory

LiftBros launches in Worcestershire. The 70 gyms in `LiftBros/Core/Data/WorcestershireGyms.swift` were researched in November 2026 by four research agents, one per area: Worcester; Wychavon & Malvern Hills; Wyre Forest; Bromsgrove & Redditch. Sources were gym websites and directory listings (PureGym, Anytime Fitness, Everyone Active, Places Leisure, Freedom Leisure, Rivers Fitness, CrossFit.com, Hussle, gymsfitness.co.uk and others).

**Confidence**: *high* means confirmed on the gym's or operator's own site. *medium* means several directory sources agreed. *low* means a single directory listing: the gym may have moved, closed or be private. **Check low-confidence entries before launch.** Blank postcodes couldn't be confirmed and weren't guessed.

Known gaps and notes from the research:
- No Gym Group, Snap Fitness, JD Gyms, Bannatyne or Everlast was found in Worcester city. The Gym Group's Worcestershire site is in Kidderminster.
- Fitness4Less Worcester now trades as **Topnotch Gyms**. **HYPR Fitness** (Weavers Wharf, Kidderminster) is reported permanently closed and is excluded.
- Some entries may be duplicates or mislistings, notably Anytime Fitness on Droitwich Road, Worcester, which was dropped. Foundry Gym Bromsgrove was reported as "not yet open" in 2024.
- Retirement-village and school-only facilities (Richmond Villages Wood Norton, Sport Martley) were excluded because public membership couldn't be confirmed.
- Coverage of villages (Broadway, Ombersley, Wolverley, Alvechurch, Wythall) is thin.

To add or correct a gym, edit `WorcestershireGyms.swift`. Ids are slugs of the name and must stay stable once live, because profiles reference them.
